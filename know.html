About the College Voting System

This online voting system allows students to vote securely and transparently for their representatives. The system ensures fairness, authentication, and ease of use.

Election Rules & Guidelines

  • Voter Eligibility: Only approved and registered students with valid PRN and Roll Numbers are eligible to vote.
  • One Voter, One Vote: Every eligible student can cast only one vote per position. Duplicate voting is prevented by the system.
  • Confidentiality & Security: All cast votes are completely anonymous and securely encrypted. Do not share your account credentials mapping to your student ID.
  • Campaign Guidelines: Candidates must strictly follow election campaign protocols. Malpractice will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Dispute Resolution: The Election Commission's decision on any voting disputes, counting discrepancies, and final results declaration is absolute and final.
